How this page works

A plain-language guide to everything on this page. Nothing here changes when you tap around — it's just reference.

The basics

Both of you can edit this at once

This isn't a document you email back and forth — it's one shared page. When either of you changes something, the other person sees it appear automatically, usually within a second or two, without needing to refresh.

How to edit a field

Tap directly on the text you want to change (a title, a note, a checklist item) and start typing. To save it, either press Enter/Return or just tap somewhere else on the screen. You'll see it flash a soft green for a moment — that's your confirmation it saved. If you don't see the flash, the edit may not have registered — try again.

"Your name" box (top of the page)

Type your name in here once. It's only used to label the "last edited by" note at the top, so you can both tell at a glance who made the most recent change. It doesn't create an account or log you in to anything.

The dot next to "Synced"

Teal/green means your changes have reached the shared copy and the other person can see them. If it says you're offline, an orange banner appears at the top — you can still look at everything, but new edits won't save until you're back online.

Add this page to your phone's home screen

Open this link in your phone's browser, then use the browser's "Add to Home Screen" option (in Safari: the Share button, then "Add to Home Screen"; in Chrome: the ⋮ menu, then "Add to Home screen"). It'll then open like a regular app icon — no app store install needed.

The badge under the trip title

Counts down the days until the trip starts. Once you're on the trip it switches to "Day 2 of 4" style, and afterwards it says the trip is complete — all worked out automatically from the dates in the Itinerary tab.

Itinerary tab

Changing a stop's date

Tap the small date box on a card to change the day. The bold weekday and date shown above it update by themselves — you only ever need to touch the small box.

Typing in a location

Type a place name (a town, a landmark, an address — whatever you'd search for) into the Location field and tap away. The page looks it up and quietly fills in a "Navigate in Waze" link for that spot. If it can't find the place, a small note appears telling you so — tap "± coordinates" underneath to type in map coordinates by hand instead, if you have them.

Weather forecast

Shows up automatically on a card once that stop has a location (or coordinates) and its date is within about two weeks away. Further out than that, forecasts aren't reliable yet, so nothing shows.

Drive time to the next stop

Once a stop and the one after it both have a location filled in, the card shows roughly how long the drive is and how far, so you can sanity-check the day's plan.

Reordering stops on the same day

If you have two or more stops planned for the same date, small ▲/▼ arrows let you move one earlier or later in that day's running order. They only affect stops sharing that same date — they won't move a stop to a different day (change the date field for that).

Adding or removing a day

"+ Add another day" at the bottom adds a blank stop to fill in. "Remove" on a card deletes that stop — there's no undo, so double-check before tapping it.

Map tab

What it shows

Every stop that has a location (or coordinates) filled in, plotted on a map and joined by a line in date order — a quick way to see the shape of the trip.

Checklists tab

Six separate lists

4 weeks out, 2 weeks out, Longfen's packing, Gwen's packing, the day before, and on the road. Tap "+ add" under any list to add an item, tick the box as you get it done — ticked items get a line through them so it's obvious what's left.

Budget tab

Entering figures

Fill in a budgeted and an actual amount for each category (Lodging, Transport, Food, Activities, Misc). The totals row at the bottom adds itself up — you never need to calculate it yourself.

"Figures entered in" dropdown

Tells the page what currency the numbers above are in. If you pick something other than SGD, a line appears underneath showing roughly what those totals are in SGD, using that day's exchange rate — useful for keeping a mental sense of the cost in home-currency terms, though it's a reference figure, not a precise conversion. If your currency isn't in the list, choose "Other…" and type its 3-letter code (e.g. VND) — the conversion line just won't show if that code has no reference rate.

Contacts and Extras tabs

Contacts

Phone numbers, booking references, or anything else you might need to look up quickly while on the road — one card per contact.

Extras

A catch-all for anything that doesn't fit elsewhere — pet-sitter instructions, medication reminders, whatever's specific to this trip.

Bookings tab

Flights, Accommodations, Car Rental

Confirmed booking details, kept separate from the day-by-day plan in Itinerary so you can find a confirmation number or flight time quickly without scrolling through the whole trip. Each card holds one flight leg, one stay, or one rental — tap "+ Add" under any section for a blank one, and cards list themselves in date order automatically.

Sharing this page

The Share button (top right)

Shows a QR code and a "Copy link" button for this exact page, so you can quickly send it to someone else or scan it on another device.

Print / Save PDF

Turns the page into a clean, printable version (hides the tabs and buttons) if you'd like a paper copy or a PDF — your browser or phone's own print/save-as-PDF option handles the rest.

Anyone with this link can edit it

There's no password or login — whoever has this exact web address can view and change everything on it. Only share the link with people you'd trust to edit the trip.

If you edit while offline

You can still tap around and edit while offline — an orange banner says so, and tells you how many edits are waiting. Nothing is lost: those edits are saved on your phone and sent automatically the moment you're back online, even if you close the page in between.
